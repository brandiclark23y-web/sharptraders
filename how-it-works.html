How It Works

Your journey to automated trading in 7 simple steps

01

Choose Your EA

Browse our marketplace and select an Expert Advisor that matches your trading strategy and risk tolerance. Each EA has detailed information about its performance, strategy, and requirements.

02

Review Information

Examine the EA's specifications, performance metrics, supported platforms, and risk level. All performance data is clearly labeled to help you make an informed decision.

03

Chat with Seller

Have questions? Use our built-in chat system to communicate directly with the seller. Discuss the EA's performance, setup requirements, and any other concerns you might have.

04

Create an Order

Once you're satisfied with your choice, the seller will create an order for you. You'll see the details and can proceed with payment.

05

Complete Payment

Pay securely through our payment system. Your payment information is never stored on our servers.

06

Receive Your EA

After payment confirmation, you'll receive immediate access to download your EA file along with installation instructions and documentation.

07

Install and Test

Install the EA on your MetaTrader platform and test it on a demo account first. This allows you to understand its behavior without risking real funds.
